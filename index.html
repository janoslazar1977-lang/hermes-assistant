Hermes Assistant

A private, single-user personal assistant application.

What this app is

Hermes Assistant is a personal-use software application operated by one individual for their own private purposes. It is not a commercial product, it is not offered to the public, and it has exactly one user — the owner of the Google Account that authorises it.

The application connects to that one user's own Google Account in order to help them organise their own information: reading and sending their own email, managing their own calendar events, and creating and storing documents, spreadsheets and files in their own Google Drive.

Who can use it

Only the account owner. There is no sign-up, no public access, and no other users. The app requests access to no Google data other than the owner's own.

How your data is handled

  • Data accessed through Google APIs is used solely to carry out the task the owner asked for.
  • Data is never sold, rented, shared, or disclosed to any third party.
  • Data is never used for advertising, profiling, or machine-learning training.
  • Data is never transferred to anyone other than the account owner.
  • Credentials and access tokens are stored only on the owner's own equipment.
