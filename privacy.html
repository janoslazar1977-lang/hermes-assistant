Privacy Policy

Application: Hermes Assistant  |  Last updated: 22 October 2026

1. Scope

This policy describes how the Hermes Assistant application handles information obtained from Google APIs. Hermes Assistant is a private, personal-use application with a single user: the owner of the Google Account that authorises it.

2. What the application accesses

With the account owner's explicit permission, the application may access:

  • Gmail — to read, search, draft and send the owner's own email at the owner's request.
  • Google Calendar — to view, create and modify the owner's own calendar events.
  • Google Drive, Docs and Sheets — to create, read and edit files in the owner's own Drive.
  • Basic profile — the owner's name and email address, to identify the account.

3. How the information is used

Information is used only to perform the specific task the owner has requested. It is not used for any other purpose. The application performs no profiling, no analytics, no behavioural tracking and no advertising.

4. Sharing and disclosure

The application does not:

  • sell, rent, trade or otherwise share information with third parties;
  • transfer information to advertisers, data brokers or marketing platforms;
  • disclose information to any party other than the account owner, except where required by law.

5. Storage and security

The application runs on equipment controlled by the account owner. OAuth credentials and access tokens are stored on that equipment and are used only to authenticate to the owner's own Google Account. No Google user data is stored on any server operated by a third party.

6. Retention and deletion

Information is retained only for as long as needed to complete the requested task. The account owner may revoke the application's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately ends all access. Deleting the application's stored credentials removes any locally held tokens.

7. Limited Use disclosure

Hermes Assistant's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

8. Children

This application is not directed to children and is not used by anyone other than its single adult owner.

9. Changes to this policy

Any change to this policy will be published on this page, with the "Last updated" date above revised.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or about data handling can be sent to janoslazar@hotmail.com.
